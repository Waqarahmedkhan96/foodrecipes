An Eid morning table

Sheer khurma before prayers, kebabs by noon. A menu plan for hosting twelve without missing the day.

Eid morning has its own order. Sheer khurma before prayers, guests by mid-morning and a full table by lunch. With a little planning, the cook gets to enjoy the day too.

Two days before

Make anything that improves with time. Chicken qorma tastes deeper on the second day, and gulab jamun want a long soak in syrup. Chop and fry a big batch of onions and keep them in an airtight jar for garnishing everything from qorma to zarda.

The night before

Cook the fruit custard and chill it, but keep the fruit aside. Colour and parboil the rice for zarda so it only needs steaming in the morning. Set the table, iron the dastarkhwan and lay out the serving bowls with a note on each so helpers know what goes where.

Eid morning

Warm the sheer khurma first so it is ready when the family returns from prayers. While guests arrive, steam the zarda, warm the qorma gently and fold the fruit into the custard just before it goes on the table.

Fatma's tipKeep one hob free all day for chai. On Eid, the kettle never stops.

A menu for twelve

Sheer khurma to start, then shahi chicken qorma with naan, a beef or chicken biryani, raita and green chutney. For something sweet, zarda and fruit custard. It looks generous, but most of it is made ahead.
