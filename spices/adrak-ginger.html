Adrak (ginger)

Adrak lehsan ka paste is the first step of so many Pakistani recipes that most kitchens keep a jar ready in the fridge.

Urdu nameAdrak
EnglishGinger
Botanical nameZingiber officinale
Try it inLahori chicken karahi, Royal beef nihari, Chicken tikka

What is adrak?

Adrak is the fresh rhizome (underground stem) of Zingiber officinale. Dried and ground, it is called sonth. Its warm, peppery bite comes from compounds called gingerols, which turn into gentler shogaols when ginger is dried or cooked.

An ancient root

Ginger is native to warm parts of Asia and has been used in food and folk medicine for thousands of years. Researchers studying Indus Valley sites such as Farmana and Harappa found evidence of curries made with ginger, garlic and turmeric.

Why we use it in Pakistani cooking

Ginger-garlic paste is fried after the onions in most salan, karahi, qorma and biryani. Fine matchsticks of fresh adrak garnish karahi, nihari and haleem. Ginger also goes into chai on cold days and into the marinade for tikka.

How to use it well

Peel ginger with the edge of a spoon. Make paste with equal parts ginger and garlic and a little oil and salt; it keeps a week in the fridge or months frozen in ice-cube trays. Fry the paste until the raw smell goes, about a minute.

What science says about ginger

According to NCCIH, research suggests ginger may help with nausea and vomiting in pregnancy, and ginger supplements might reduce the severity of menstrual cramps. It's uncertain whether it helps with nausea from chemotherapy or surgery, and most studies haven't found it helpful for motion sickness. Many ginger studies are small or of low quality.

Safety

Ginger in food is safe for most people. Large amounts can cause heartburn or stomach upset. Ginger can interact with some medicines, so check with a doctor before taking supplements, especially if you are pregnant.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use dried ginger instead of fresh?

Yes, but it tastes different. Use about ¼ teaspoon ground ginger for each tablespoon of fresh.

Is ginger good for nausea?

Research suggests it may help nausea in pregnancy, but ask your doctor before using supplements.

How do I store ginger-garlic paste?

In a clean, airtight jar in the fridge for up to a week, or frozen in ice-cube trays for several months.
