Spice guide · Piper nigrum

Kali mirch (black pepper)

Long before the chilli arrived, kali mirch was the heat of the subcontinent. It was so valuable that it was once called black gold.

Urdu nameKali mirch
EnglishBlack pepper
Botanical namePiper nigrum
Try it inPeshawari beef karahi, Shinwari chicken karahi, Shinwari dumba karahi

What is kali mirch?

Kali mirch is the dried berry of Piper nigrum, a climbing vine native to South Asia's Malabar coast and Western Ghats. Black peppercorns are picked unripe, briefly cooked and dried until wrinkled and dark. White pepper is the seed with its outer skin removed. Its bite comes from piperine.

The spice that built empires

Peppercorns were found in the nostrils of the Egyptian pharaoh Ramesses II, placed there during mummification after his death in 1213 BC. Rome bought so much pepper from India that the writer Pliny the Elder complained about the drain on the empire's gold. The search for pepper and other spices helped drive the age of sea exploration.

Why we use it in Pakistani cooking

Whole peppercorns are one of the khada masala (whole spices) in pulao, biryani and yakhni. Freshly crushed pepper is the main seasoning in Peshawari and Shinwari karahi, where powdered spices are skipped. It is also part of garam masala and chaat masala.

How to use it well

Crush or grind peppercorns just before using: ground pepper loses its aroma quickly. Add coarse pepper near the end of cooking for the strongest flavour.

What science says about black pepper

Piperine is best known for helping the body absorb other compounds. In a 1998 study, taking 20 mg of piperine with 2 g of curcumin raised curcumin absorption in human volunteers by about 2,000%. This is why haldi and kali mirch are a classic pair. Other claimed benefits of pepper come mostly from laboratory research and need human studies.

Safety

Pepper is safe in food amounts. Piperine can change how the body absorbs some medicines, so talk to your doctor before taking piperine or black pepper supplements.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between black and white pepper?

Black pepper is the whole dried unripe berry. White pepper is the seed with the outer skin removed, giving a milder, earthier taste.

Why is it called the king of spices?

For centuries it was one of the most traded and valuable spices in the world.

Does black pepper help turmeric?

Yes. Piperine increased curcumin absorption by about 2,000% in a human study.
