Spice guide · Syzygium aromaticum

Laung (cloves)

Small, dark and powerful, a few laung are enough to scent a whole pot of pulao.

Urdu nameLaung
EnglishCloves
Botanical nameSyzygium aromaticum
Try it inBeef biryani, Yakhni chicken pulao, Matar pulao

What is laung?

Laung are the dried flower buds of Syzygium aromaticum, a tree native to the Maluku Islands of Indonesia, the original Spice Islands. Their strong, sweet and slightly numbing taste comes from eugenol, which makes up most of clove oil.

From the Spice Islands to the world

Cloves once grew on only a few islands, yet they travelled astonishingly far. Archaeologists found cloves in a house in Terqa, Syria, dated to around 1720 BC, and they reached Rome by the first century AD. The fight to control the clove trade shaped colonial history in South-east Asia.

Why we use it in Pakistani cooking

Laung is part of the khada masala in biryani, pulao, yakhni and qorma, and of garam masala.

How to use it well

Use 3 to 6 cloves for a family pot. Fry them in oil until they swell slightly. Too many make food taste medicinal and numb the tongue.

What science says about cloves

Eugenol may have anti-inflammatory and antibacterial effects, and clove oil has a long use as a home remedy for toothache. Cleveland Clinic notes that there isn't enough scientific evidence to support cloves for medical purposes, and many studies are in the laboratory or in animals.

Safety

Whole cloves in cooking are safe for most people. Clove oil is very different: it can irritate the gums and skin, and swallowing even small amounts can seriously harm children, so keep it out of their reach. Eugenol can affect blood clotting, so people on blood thinners or planning surgery should speak to a doctor before using clove products.

Frequently asked questions

Are cloves the same as laung?

Yes. Laung is the Urdu name for cloves.

Does clove help toothache?

It is a traditional remedy and may numb the gums briefly, but see a dentist for tooth pain. Never give clove oil to children.

How many cloves go in a biryani?

Usually 4 to 6 for a pot that serves six.
