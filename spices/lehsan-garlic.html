Spice guide · Allium sativum

Lehsan (garlic)

Golden slivers of lehsan sizzling in ghee make a plain bowl of daal irresistible.

Urdu nameLehsan
EnglishGarlic
Botanical nameAllium sativum
Try it inDaal chana, Daal masoor, Lahori chicken karahi

What is lehsan?

Lehsan is the bulb of Allium sativum, a relative of onions and leeks. When garlic is crushed or chopped, it forms allicin, the compound behind its sharp smell. Cooking mellows it into something sweet and nutty.

A spice as old as cooking

Garlic has been grown and eaten for thousands of years. Ancient writings from Egypt, Greece and India describe its use as food and medicine, and researchers have found evidence of garlic in Indus Valley cooking.

Why we use it in Pakistani cooking

Garlic is half of the ginger-garlic paste that starts most curries. Sliced garlic fried in ghee is the classic tarka for daal chana and daal masoor. It also goes into marinades, achar and chutneys.

How to use it well

Fry sliced garlic on medium heat until just golden, because burnt garlic turns bitter. For the strongest flavour, crush garlic and leave it for a few minutes before cooking. Store whole bulbs in a cool, dry, airy place, not the fridge.

What science says about garlic

NCCIH says cholesterol is the best-studied use: garlic supplements may lower total and LDL cholesterol a little in people with high cholesterol. Limited evidence suggests supplements may modestly lower blood pressure in people with high blood pressure and blood sugar in people with diabetes. There's very little research on garlic and colds.

Safety

Garlic in food is safe for most people, apart from garlic breath. Garlic supplements may increase the risk of bleeding, so tell your doctor if you take them, especially with blood thinners or aspirin or before surgery. Never put raw garlic on the skin, as it can cause burns.

Frequently asked questions

Does garlic lower blood pressure?

Supplements may lower it slightly in people with high blood pressure, but they don't replace prescribed treatment.

Why does my fried garlic taste bitter?

It was cooked too hot or too long. Take it off the heat as soon as it turns light gold.

How do I get rid of garlic breath?

Chewing fresh mint, parsley or a cardamom pod after the meal helps.
