Spice guide · Cuminum cyminum

Zeera (cumin)

The crackle of zeera in hot ghee is the first sound of countless Pakistani meals, from the tarka on daal to the base of pulao.

Urdu nameZeera
EnglishCumin
Botanical nameCuminum cyminum
Try it inDaal masoor, Zeera raita, Matar pulao

What is zeera?

Zeera is the dried seed of Cuminum cyminum, a small flowering plant in the parsley family. The seeds are long and ridged, with a warm, earthy, slightly bitter flavour that comes mainly from an aromatic compound called cuminaldehyde.

A spice that travelled with civilisation

Cumin probably began in Central or South-western Asia or the eastern Mediterranean. Wild cumin seeds have been found at Atlit-Yam, a submerged settlement on the Israeli coast dated to around 6000 BC, and cumin seeds have been excavated in Syria from the second millennium BC. Ancient Egyptians used it as a spice and in mummification. Today the subcontinent grows most of the world's cumin.

Why we use it in Pakistani cooking

Whole zeera is fried first, in ghee or oil, to release its oils: this is the start of matar pulao, yakhni and many sabzis. It is the heart of the tarka poured over daal. Roasted and ground, it seasons raita, chaat and lassi. Together with dhaniya it forms the backbone of garam masala and almost every curry blend.

How to use it well

Fry whole seeds until they darken one shade and smell nutty, about 20 to 30 seconds; burnt zeera turns bitter. For bhuna zeera, dry-roast seeds in a pan until fragrant, cool, then crush. Ground cumin loses its aroma within months, so buy whole seeds and grind small batches.

What science says about cumin

A 2025 meta-analysis in Frontiers in Nutrition combined nine small randomised trials (531 adults with metabolic disorders). Cumin supplements were linked with lower fasting blood sugar and triglycerides, higher HDL (good) cholesterol and smaller waist size. The authors rated most of this evidence as low certainty, all the trials were done in one country, and they called for larger, better studies. Cumin in food is a delicious habit, not a treatment.

Safety

Cumin is safe in normal cooking amounts. Concentrated supplements and oils are not the same as cooking with the seed, so check with a doctor before using them, especially if you are pregnant or take diabetes medicine.

Frequently asked questions

Is zeera the same as jeera?

Yes. Zeera and jeera are both names for cumin seeds. Kala zeera (black cumin) is a different, smaller seed with a sweeter, smokier taste.

What is bhuna zeera?

Cumin seeds dry-roasted in a pan and then crushed. It is used in raita, chaat and drinks.

Does cumin help digestion?

It is traditionally used for digestion, and early studies are interesting, but there isn't strong evidence yet. Enjoy it as a flavour.

Sources
