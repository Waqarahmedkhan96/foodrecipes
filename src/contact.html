Can I swap chicken for mutton?

Yes, in most curries and pulaos. Mutton needs roughly twice the cooking time, so add it earlier and keep the pot covered.

Where do I find sella basmati?

Any South Asian grocer. Sella (parboiled) rice holds its shape through dum, which is why it is the Pakistani choice for biryani and pulao.

Do you take recipe requests?

Always. Choose “Recipe request” in the form. The most asked-for dishes go into the Sunday letter.
