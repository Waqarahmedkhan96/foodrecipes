My Story

A cookbook in 2009. An open kitchen today.

I’m Fatma. My love for cooking has always been closely tied to the people I cook for. There is something special about making a meal, bringing everyone to the table, and hearing someone ask, “Will you make this again?”

My food comes from Pakistan, but my home is in Denmark. For many years I have cooked our family’s Pakistani dishes in a Danish kitchen, and along the way Danish baking found its place at our table too: a tray of drømmekage, a carrot cake for a birthday, a berry tart in summer. That is why you will find both here, Pakistani flavours and Danish bakes side by side, just as they are in my home.

For years, my children and friends encouraged me to share my Pakistani recipes. Writing a cookbook was a dream I had carried for a long time, but it was my son’s encouragement—and his offer to help—that finally gave me the courage to begin.

In 2009, that dream became a published cookbook.

It meant so much to hold those recipes in my hands, knowing that the dishes I had made with love could now find their way into other people’s homes. I wanted readers to discover the joy of Pakistani cooking and feel that they could make these dishes themselves.

Over the years, people continued to ask for my recipes and copies of the book. But with only a few copies left, I couldn’t keep sharing it with everyone who wanted one.

That stayed with me. I had written the book to share my cooking, and I still had so much I wanted to share.

So I decided to give my recipes a new home.

That is how this website came to be. Here at Fatma’s Tasty Pakistan, I’m bringing the recipes from my cookbook online, alongside more dishes from my kitchen, so you can return to them whenever you want to cook.

Perhaps you are learning to make Pakistani food for the first time. Perhaps you miss a familiar flavour, or simply want something new to put on the dinner table. Whatever brings you here, you are welcome.

You don’t need to get everything perfect. Start with a dish that makes you hungry, take your time, and make it your own. I hope these recipes give you the confidence to cook, the pleasure of sharing a meal, and a few favourites you will return to for years.

My family encouraged me to write the book. The people who kept asking for it inspired me to create this space.

In 2009, I shared my recipes through the pages of a book. Today, I’m sharing them here—with a place for you at the table.

With love,

Fatma
