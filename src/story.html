Fatma's story

“Every recipe here was first cooked for family, long before it was written for you.”

Tasty Pakistan began as a notebook of my mother's recipes, the measurements written as “a handful” and “until it smells right”. I cooked each one until I could write it down properly.

This is a place for inspiration, not a restaurant or a shop. Come for the recipes, stay for the stories behind them, and cook something that reminds you of home.
