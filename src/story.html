About Fatma

Before there was a cookbook, there was a family who believed in my cooking.

For years, my children and friends encouraged me to share the food I loved to make. Then one day, my son said something that brought a long-held dream a little closer: “You should write a cookbook. We’ll help you.”

Those simple words meant so much. What had begun as meals made for the people closest to me became something I could share with people I had never met.

In 2009, that dream became my Pakistani cookbook—a collection of recipes prepared with love, written to welcome others into the food and traditions so dear to me.

To me, cooking has never been only about ingredients. It is a way of caring for someone. It is the familiar aroma that makes you feel at home, the invitation to sit a little longer, and the quiet happiness of seeing someone enjoy what you have made.

That is the spirit behind Fatma’s kitchen.

Through my recipes, I want to make Pakistani cooking feel welcoming and possible in your own home. Whether you are discovering these flavours for the first time or returning to dishes you have always loved, I hope you find something here that brings you closer to the people around your table.

My children gave me the courage to share my recipes. Every person who cooks one gives that story a new place to continue.

From my kitchen to yours, with love.
